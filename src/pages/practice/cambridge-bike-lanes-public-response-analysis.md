whatIs: >-
  


  **This practice focuses on analyzing public discourse using clear and specific AI prompts. The task involves examining 40 comments from a news article about the proposed expansion of protected bike lanes in Cambridge. The goal is to use an AI system to identify the main arguments, public sentiment, recurring concerns, stakeholder perspectives, and areas of disagreement.**


  **The practice also demonstrates how improving a prompt through clear instructions can produce a more structured, detailed, and useful response.**
whyDo: >-
  


  **This practice helps develop the ability to write clear and effective prompts when working with AI. By providing specific instructions about the task, the context, the information to analyze, and the desired format of the response, users can obtain more relevant and useful results.**


  **It also develops skills in analyzing public discourse, identifying patterns in different opinions, distinguishing between facts and opinions, and synthesizing large amounts of qualitative information into insights that can support decision-making. In this case, the final analysis is designed to help prepare a professional briefing for Mayor Denise Simmons about public reactions to the proposed bike lane expansion.**
howTo: >-
  


  **1. Read the news article and copy the 40 comments related to the proposed expansion of protected bike lanes in Cambridge.**


  **2. Start a new thread in PingPong Classic.**


  **3. Paste the 40 comments into the prompt as the reference material to be analyzed.**


  **4. Use clear and specific instructions to ask the AI to analyze the public discourse.**


  **5. Ask the AI to identify the overall public sentiment, arguments in favor, arguments against, recurring concerns, stakeholder perspectives, and major areas of disagreement.**


  **6. Request that the response be organized with clear headings and bullet points so it can be used in a professional briefing for Mayor Denise Simmons.**


  **7. Review the response and refine the prompt if necessary to improve clarity, structure, and usefulness.**


  **8. Compare the improved response with the original approach and reflect on how writing clear instructions affected the quality of the AI's analysis.**
